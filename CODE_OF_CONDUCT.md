# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want this project to be a respectful, inclusive and professional place to collaborate. Participants are expected to make technical disagreement evidence-based, protect privacy, and treat people fairly regardless of background, identity, experience, language, nationality, disability, religion, age or other personal characteristic.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, direct and constructive.
- Critique the work and evidence, not the person.
- Welcome questions and explain assumptions without belittling others.
- Respect names, pronouns, language and accessibility needs.
- Protect confidential, personal and security-sensitive information.
- Credit contributors and sources accurately.
- Accept correction and take responsibility for mistakes.
- Stop behavior when a moderator or affected participant asks that it stop.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, stalking or unwanted sexual attention.
- Discriminatory, demeaning or deliberately exclusionary language or imagery.
- Personal attacks, trolling, sustained disruption or bad-faith argument.
- Publishing another person's private information or project confidential data.
- Pressuring someone to disclose credentials, identity, employer/customer data or private communications.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith.
- Using project access to test or change live merchant/customer/vendor systems outside explicit authorization.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, reviews, discussions, project communication and public spaces where a participant represents the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through a trusted private channel shown on the owner's profile. Do not include unrelated personal data or publish sensitive evidence in a public issue. Security vulnerabilities follow [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers will review reports as impartially and confidentially as practical. They may remove content, issue a private warning, limit participation, temporarily suspend access or permanently exclude a participant based on severity and pattern. Knowingly false reports or retaliation may also result in action; an unproven good-faith report will not.

## Privacy

Report details are shared only with people needed to assess and respond, subject to safety and legal obligations. Keep records no longer than necessary for a fair response.
